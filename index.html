📚 Standard Book Library

Suggested academic references. Confirm the relevant edition, chapter and depth against your prescribed syllabus.

🔬

Pathology

Robbins & Cotran — Pathologic Basis of Disease

Foundational pathology reference. Some chapters may be more advanced than required for BPT.

💊

Pharmacology

K. D. Tripathi — Essentials of Medical Pharmacology

Reference for general pharmacology and drug classes.

🏃

Exercise Therapy

Kisner, Colby & Borstad — Therapeutic Exercise: Foundations and Techniques

Reference for therapeutic exercise principles and techniques.

🦴

Biomechanics

Nordin & Frankel — Basic Biomechanics of the Musculoskeletal System

Reference for musculoskeletal mechanics and movement analysis.

This is an initial reference list, not a verified complete BPT reading list. Kinequill's final library should map each syllabus topic to suitable references and legally accessible resources. It should not reproduce entire copyrighted textbooks without permission.
